FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will agents send anything without my approval?

Never. Agents prepare drafts and wait for your approval before anything is sent.

What can the agents access?

Only the tools you connect, and only with the permissions you choose. Research agents can stay read-only.

How many teammates do I get?

You start with seven, and you can add your own teammates any time.

Do I need technical skills?

No. You talk to your Chief of Staff in plain language, the way you would a colleague.
